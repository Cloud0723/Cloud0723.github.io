Building reliable and efficient learning systems for language models and agents.

I am a Ph.D. candidate at Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Alfredo Garcia. My research focuses on LLM post-training and reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on fully asynchronous training, long-horizon agents, and joint reward–policy learning.

I work across algorithms, theory, and training systems. Previously, I was a research intern at ByteDance and GE HealthCare, working on asynchronous RL and multi-turn agent optimization.
